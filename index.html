Our Story

An Atelier Obsessed With The Details.

Maison Luxe was founded on a simple belief: everyday sneakers should feel as considered as a tailored garment. We work with tanneries in Italy and Portugal, and finish each pair by hand in our own factory.

We supply independent boutiques, curated online retailers and hospitality groups across the United States. Private label, custom colorways and low MOQ wholesale are available on request.
